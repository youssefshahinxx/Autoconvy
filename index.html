The Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Click each question to reveal the answer.
What exactly is an AI agent, and how is it different from a chatbot? ↓

A chatbot follows a script. It presents options and waits for button clicks. An AI agent understands natural language, adapts to the conversation, makes decisions mid-call, and executes real actions — like booking an appointment, updating a CRM record, or routing a request based on urgency. The difference isn't cosmetic. It's the difference between a form and a conversation.

Do I need to switch my existing software or tools? ↓

No. We build around what you already use. If your team runs on a specific CRM, calendar, or communication platform, the agent connects to it — not the other way around. We don't ask businesses to change their workflows. We automate them.

Who owns the agent after it's built? ↓

The system is built specifically for your business, in your brand voice, connected to your tools. You own the outcomes. However, the underlying infrastructure (the platforms, workflows, and configurations) is managed by Autoconvey as part of the monthly service. If you cancel, we provide full documentation of everything we've built.

How do usage costs work? What am I actually paying? ↓

There are two layers. The first is Autoconvey's monthly service fee — this covers design, management, monitoring, and optimization. The second is infrastructure usage, billed at cost and passed through transparently: things like voice minutes, workflow executions, and telephony. You pay exactly what you consume. Nothing is marked up. A full breakdown is in the pricing section.

How long does it take to go live? ↓

For a standard Essentials setup (one inbound agent, one workflow, CRM and calendar integration), we target 10–14 business days from the onboarding call to live deployment. More complex builds with multiple agents and workflows take 3–5 weeks depending on integration requirements.

What happens if the agent makes a mistake or gives wrong information? ↓

We build with guardrails. The agent is scoped specifically to what it knows — your services, your pricing, your booking logic. When a question falls outside its scope, it acknowledges that and either escalates to a human or takes a message. No hallucinating answers. We also monitor calls and provide monthly quality reports. If something performs below standard, we fix it.

Can the agent handle calls in languages other than English? ↓

Yes. Multi-language support is available depending on the voice platform and configuration. If your customer base includes Spanish-speaking callers (or others), we configure the agent to detect language and respond accordingly. Mention this in your onboarding questionnaire.

What if I want to cancel? ↓

No lock-in contracts. Cancel any time with 30 days' written notice. We'll provide documentation of everything we've built so you can hand it off or rebuild elsewhere. We'd rather earn your business month to month than hold it hostage.

Do you work with businesses outside the US? ↓

Yes. The platforms we use (RetellAI, n8n, and others) support international deployment. Telephony costs and availability vary by country. Book a discovery call to discuss your location and we'll confirm what's possible.

I don't have a CRM. Can you still build an agent for me? ↓

Yes. We can configure the agent to log interactions into a Google Sheet, a ClickUp workspace, or another lightweight system until you're ready for a full CRM. We can also help set up a basic CRM pipeline as part of an add-on service.

What industries do you work with? ↓

We focus on any business where communication volume is high, response time matters, and repetitive tasks (booking, qualifying, routing) consume staff hours. This includes home services, healthcare and dental, real estate, logistics, legal, hospitality, and retail — but the model applies broadly. If your phone rings and your team handles repetitive inquiries, we can help.

Is my customer data secure? ↓

Yes. We use enterprise-grade platforms with encryption in transit and at rest. Autoconvey does not store or sell your customer data. All call recordings (if enabled) are retained within the platforms you're already subscribed to, under your account ownership. Our full data handling policy is in the Privacy Policy below.
